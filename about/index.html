About BeeN Tools

Small, fast, free tools for everyday life, made with care.

What BeeN Tools is

BeeN Tools is a small collection of web tools that solve everyday problems without making you install anything. It started with a bill calculator built to split the monthly rent and shared household bills fairly, and it keeps growing one useful tool at a time.

Every tool lives on its own page under this one website. They share the same look, the same dark and light themes, and the same Google sign-in, so using a second tool feels familiar from the first minute.

Our tools

🧮 BeeN CalculatorSplit rent and shared bills between people, round the result, and keep a history of every month. 📈 Fiverr TrackerTrack organic orders, ads and self paid orders across profiles, with Fiverr fees worked out for you.

How we build

  • Free to use. The tools are free. If advertising is shown on the site, it is what pays for keeping everything online.
  • Fast and light. The tools are plain web pages that load quickly on a phone or a laptop.
  • Your data stays yours. Most of what you type is saved only in your own browser. If you choose to sign in with Google, your records are stored under your own account so you can open them on another device. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
  • Honest numbers. Where a tool uses a formula, such as marketplace fees, we show how the result was worked out and let you change the settings.

Get in touch

Found a bug, or have an idea for the next tool? We read every message. Head to the Contact page and tell us what would make BeeN Tools more useful for you.
