Contact us

Questions, bug reports or ideas for the next tool? Here is how to reach us.

Email us

The best way to reach us is by email: contact@example.com. We aim to reply within a few working days.

What to include

  • Bug report: the tool you were using, what you expected, what happened instead, and your browser and device.
  • Feature or tool idea: the problem you want solved and how you would use it day to day.
  • Privacy request: the email address of the Google account you signed in with, and whether you want your data exported or deleted. See the Privacy Policy.
Please never send passwords or payment details. We will never ask for them. BeeN Tools is not affiliated with Fiverr or any other marketplace, so we cannot help with orders or accounts on those platforms.
