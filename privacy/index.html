Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information BeeN Tools ("we", "us") handles when you use this website and its tools, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information we handle

  • Data you enter into a tool. Bill items, order entries, profile names and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your device so the tools remember them.
  • Google sign-in (optional for the BeeN Calculator, required for the Fiverr Tracker). We use Firebase Authentication from Google. When you sign in we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID. Your tool data is then also stored in our Firebase Firestore database under that ID so it syncs between your devices. Only you, signed in to your account, can read it through the tools.
  • Preferences. Your light or dark theme and similar settings are stored locally in your browser.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider and Google services may receive standard request information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, as part of delivering the site.

How we use information

We use it only to run the tools: to show your records, keep them in sync, and keep the site secure and working. We do not sell your personal data and we do not read your tool entries for any other purpose.

Cookies and advertising

The site may display advertising from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or through aboutads.info.
  • Where we show an ad consent banner, you can choose personalised or non-personalised ads. You can change that choice later by clearing this site's saved data in your browser.
  • See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

The login-only Fiverr Tracker does not display ads.

Third-party services

The site relies on Google Firebase (sign-in and database) and, when enabled, Google AdSense. These providers have their own privacy policies and handle data under their terms.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use the BeeN Calculator without signing in.
  • You can ask us to export or delete the data stored under your account by emailing contact@example.com.
  • Clearing your browser data removes everything stored locally on that device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us and we will help.

Children

BeeN Tools is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@example.com or use the Contact page.
