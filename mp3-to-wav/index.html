Why convert MP3 to WAV?

  • Editing and mixing. Video editors and audio editors such as Audacity, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and music production software work most smoothly with WAV.
  • Compatibility. Some games, hardware players, samplers and older programs accept only WAV.
  • No extra quality loss. WAV is uncompressed, so saving it again and again does not reduce quality the way re-encoding an MP3 does.

Converting does not restore detail that was removed when the MP3 was created, so the sound will match the original MP3.

Frequently asked questions

Is the MP3 to WAV converter free?

Yes. BeeN Converter is completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many files you convert.

Are my audio files uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs inside your browser on your own device, so your files are never sent to a server. That also makes it fast, because there is nothing to upload or download first.

Does converting MP3 to WAV improve the sound quality?

No. An MP3 has already lost some audio detail when it was made, and a WAV cannot bring that back. Converting gives you an uncompressed file that is safe to edit and works everywhere, but the quality stays the same as the MP3.

Which bit depth should I choose: 16, 24 or 32?

16-bit is CD quality and works with almost everything. 24-bit gives extra headroom when you edit or mix. 32-bit float is for professional audio software. For a normal MP3, 16-bit is fine.

Why is the WAV file so much bigger than the MP3?

WAV stores raw, uncompressed audio. A three minute stereo song is about 3 MB as a 128 kbps MP3 and about 30 MB as a 16-bit WAV at 44.1 kHz, roughly ten times larger.

Can I convert many MP3 files at once?

Yes. Drop as many files as you like, press Convert, then download them one by one or all together in a single ZIP file.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, it works in modern browsers on phones, tablets and computers. Very large files can be slow on older phones because they need a lot of memory.

Which other formats can it convert to WAV?

Besides MP3, it can convert M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC and WAV files to WAV. What works depends on the audio formats your browser can play.
